Questions

Is Noor free?

Yes. Reading and listening to all 114 surahs, word-by-word meanings, Assamese pronunciation and translation, and the first 4 chapters of Learn to read are free for everyone. Recite & check, Memorisation and Smart review are also free for Al-Fatiha and the 10 shortest surahs.

What does full access include?

A one-time payment of ₹49 (India) or US$2.99 unlocks Recite & check, Memorisation and Smart review for all 114 surahs, Learn to read chapters 5–8, offline downloads, backup files and sync across devices. It is not a subscription.

Does Noor have the Quran in Assamese?

Yes. Every verse has the Assamese translation by Shaykh Rafeequl Islam Habibur Rahman, an Assamese pronunciation guide under each Arabic word, and the English translation (Saheeh International).

How does Recite & check work?

Tap the microphone on a verse and recite it aloud in Arabic. Noor listens, lights up each word as it hears it, then shows which words were right, nearly right or missed, with a match percentage. You can play back your own voice and compare it with the reciter.

Can I learn to read Arabic from the beginning?

Yes. Learn to read starts with the 28 letters and their sounds, then vowel marks, joining letters and reading rules, with audio for every letter and word.

Does it work offline and on a phone?

Noor works in any modern browser on phones and computers and can be installed like an app. With full access you can download surahs to read and listen without internet.
