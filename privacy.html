In short

Noor works without an account. Your practice stays in your own browser unless you choose to sign in with Google to sync it between your devices. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising, and there is no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your scores, daily goal, saved verses, review schedule and settings are kept in your browser’s storage.
  • Recordings of your own voice (if “Record my voice” is on) and downloaded recitations are kept only on your device and are never uploaded by this site.
  • You can delete all of it from the Progress page (“Reset progress”) or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

  • Google tells this site your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account ID. No other Google data is requested.
  • Your practice data (scores, practice history, daily goal, saved verses with notes, review schedule) is stored on the smahfuzm.in server for your account, so your other devices can receive it. Only you, signed in, can read it.
  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in on that device for up to 180 days. It is used only for this.
  • Delete it any time: Progress page → “Delete my synced data”. This removes your account and synced data from the server at once; progress already on your devices stays there.

If you buy full access (optional)

  • Payments are made on Razorpay's own pages; card, UPI and bank details go only to Razorpay.
  • Razorpay tells this site the payment ID, amount, currency, the email and phone entered on the payment page, and its status. These are stored on the smahfuzm.in server to unlock your account and to handle refunds, and are not shared.
  • See the terms and refund policy.

Other services the site uses

  • Speech recognition (Recite & check): your browser turns your voice into text using its own service — Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari. This site receives only the text.
  • Recitation audio comes from Quran.com’s audio servers, and fonts from Google Fonts. These requests include your IP address, as any web request does.
  • Google sign-in is provided by Google under Google’s privacy policy.

Children

The site is suitable for all ages. Children can use it fully without signing in.
